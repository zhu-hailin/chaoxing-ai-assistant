## 最终提示词

Create one clean logo wordmark on a genuinely TRANSPARENT BACKGROUND, no colored background or box. The only text must be exactly "学习通AI助手" in one horizontal line. All five Chinese characters and the uppercase Latin letters A I must be accurately readable in the exact order: 学 习 通 A I 助 手. Bold modern Chinese sans-serif lettering, polished typography, solid red #D93025, generously spaced and legible.

Add ONE simple thin red horizontal motion line beginning to the left of the wordmark and extending toward the right beneath the lower edge of the lettering. It may gently curve upward from the left, but must end bluntly or with a rounded cap: NO ARROWHEAD, no pointed arrow, no triangle at its end. Treat it as a restrained underline/motion stroke, not a separate symbol.

No book, star, circuit, bird, badge, enclosing circle, extra text, gradients, shadows or watermark. Centered horizontal composition with transparent margins; professional project wordmark. Exactly one logo.
